  • You choose the pace — each image stays on screen as long as you set, default 3 seconds; sub-second values like 0.33 show 3 stills per second for fast motion review (~300 ms per still is enough to judge motion)

Does it tell me which photos have motion in them?
No — and that's deliberate. It doesn't analyze your photos; it turns them into a video you can review fast, so you spot the motion. One frame per image, in order, nothing skipped.

Yes — it writes standard MP4, AVI, MKV, or WebM files. One tip: if stills blur or skip at very fast per-image times in VLC, lower the FPS or raise the seconds-per-image until each still reads clearly.
